# Changelog

What visus gained, newest first, in a few words each. One section per minor
version, headed by the last version it shipped as: a patch rewrites its minor's
heading to the new number and adds its lines there, never a section of its own.
Features only — not fixes, not why, not how; that is the README's. Three lines
to a release, six at the very most. Plain text and `code` spans only.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-27

- Open `.glb`, `.gltf` or SketchUp's `.dae` exports, and step inside them in a Meta Quest.
- Walk, teleport and turn; grab the world, or scale the model from life-size down to a miniature.
- Jump between the cameras saved in the model, and save views of your own.
- Pin notes to the model, typed in the headset or on the page; export them as Markdown or JSON.
